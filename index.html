About

Four decades in packaging

Established in 1980, Uday Multiprint manufactures printed cartons, printed boxes, blister cards, pharmaceutical inserts and other paper-based packaging. Under the guidance of Mr. Dhruv Shetty, the company has grown into a packaging partner for brands across India.

Colour is managed by a G7-certified managing partner with a background in offset printing, colour management and customer relations. We work with packaging engineers and brand managers on new launches, make mock-ups on different substrates, and suggest the value additions that suit each product. We hold ISO 9001 and FSC certifications.

“We work closely with brands so the packaging is ready when the product is. Every job should be practical, cost-effective and fully recyclable.”

Managing PartnerUday Multiprint
